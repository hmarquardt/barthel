An independent agency, established in Princeton in 1973.

For more than fifty years, The Barthel Agency has helped people in Princeton and Gibson County insure what they’ve worked for and plan for what’s next. We’re independent — which means we work for you, not for an insurance company.

Our approach

Advice before products. People before portals.

We’re a full-service insurance, employee benefits, and financial services firm. The range matters: a family that insures their home with us can plan retirement with us, and the employer who buys group health from us doesn’t have to explain their business to a stranger every year.

Being independent means we’re not stuck recommending one company’s product. We work with a variety of insurance carriers — so if we don’t have exactly the right fit, we’ll help you find the group that does.

  • Established 1973 — five decades of continuous Princeton service
  • Independent — multiple carriers, compared for your situation
  • Local staff — licensed specialists you can reach directly
  • Full range — personal, business, benefits, and financial services

Our office at 118 S. Main St — half a block south of the courthouse square.

What working with us looks like

No call centers, no ticket numbers. The way an agency worked when your parents bought their first policy — with modern tools layered on top.

1. A conversation, not a form dump

Call or stop in. We ask about your situation — family, home, work, business — then go to work shopping coverage across our carriers.

2. Options explained in English

We’ll show you the trade-offs: what’s covered, what isn’t, what it costs. You make the call; we make sure it’s an informed one.

3. Service that outlasts the sale

Policy changes, claims, life events, renewals — the relationship is the product. Most of our clients have been with us for years, and their kids are calling now too.
