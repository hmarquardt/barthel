First steps after a loss

Whatever happened — a wreck on US 41, hail on the roof, water in the basement — a few minutes of care now saves headaches later.

1. Make safety the priority

Get people out of danger first. Call 911 if anyone is hurt. For a wreck, get the other driver’s info and take photos before vehicles move if you safely can.

2. Prevent further damage

Tarp the roof, shut off the water, board the window. Carriers expect you to mitigate — keep receipts for anything you spend doing it.

3. Call us, then document

Report it to us or to your carrier’s 24-hour line, then document: photos, lists of damaged items, and any police or fire report numbers.
