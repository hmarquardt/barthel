What we cover

Every way you live in Gibson County.

  • Homeowners — dwelling, other structures, personal property, liability
  • Condo & renters — protect what the association’s master policy doesn’t
  • Landlord & rental properties — structures plus liability
  • Farm property — outbuildings, equipment, and liability
  • Umbrella liability — extra protection above home and auto

Two decisions matter most: rebuilding cost and liability limits. We’ll review both with real numbers — including whether replacement-cost or actual-cash-value coverage makes sense for your roof and contents.

Flood is separate

Standard home policies don’t cover flooding. If you’re near the Wabash or in a low area, ask us about NFIP flood options — it’s more affordable than most expect.
