The basics

Term or permanent — it depends on the job.

  • Term life — the affordable workhorse. Covers the years your family depends on your income most.
  • Whole life — builds cash value and lasts a lifetime. Fits legacy and estate goals.
  • Universal life — flexible premiums and death benefits for changing situations.
  • Long-term care planning — hybrid options that can help if care needs arrive first.

A useful rule of thumb: coverage should replace income, pay off the house, and buy your kids time — not just cover a funeral. Chris will run the numbers with you and show what each option costs.

Who needs it?

If anyone depends on your income — a spouse, kids, even parents — you probably do. Young families usually get the best rates of their lives by locking term coverage early.

Employer coverage isn’t enough

Group life through work usually ends when the job does. An individual policy travels with you.

Already have a policy?

Policies bought years ago deserve a look. Beneficiaries change, needs change, and older policies sometimes have surprises worth reviewing.
