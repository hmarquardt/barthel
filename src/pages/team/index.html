Credentials, in plain terms

Letters after a name only matter if you know what they stand for.

CFP®

Certified Financial Planner™. Requires years of experience, a comprehensive exam covering investments, tax, retirement, insurance, and estate planning, and a continuing fiduciary-style duty of care.

ChFC®

Chartered Financial Consultant®. An advanced financial-planning designation from The American College of Financial Services, with deep coursework in insurance, income taxation, and retirement planning.

CISR Elite

Certified Insurance Service Representative, Elite level. Advanced, annually updated training in personal and commercial lines — the people who know policy forms inside and out.
